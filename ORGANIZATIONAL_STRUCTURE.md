**Date**: 2026-09-11  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Timeline**: Phase D (2026 Q3) → Phase 20 (2029 Q4)  
**Strategic Focus**: Startup lean team → Enterprise organization

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the organizational growth strategy for Chess Tactics Master, from a 15-person startup team (Phase D) to a 30,000-person global enterprise (Phase 20). It covers:

1. **Organizational Structure** by phase (hierarchy, reporting lines)
2. **Hiring Plan** with budget allocation (headcount, costs)
3. **Key Roles** and responsibilities
4. **Team Composition** by function and geography
5. **Leadership Development** and succession planning
6. **Compensation Strategy** and equity allocation
7. **Company Culture** and retention initiatives

**Total Hiring**: 15 → 30,000 people over 3+ years  
**Total Compensation Budget**: $5B+ (includes salary, equity, benefits)  
**Retention Target**: 95%+ for high performers
