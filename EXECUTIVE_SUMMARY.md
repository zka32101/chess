## Risk Management

### Top 10 Risks & Mitigation

   - Risk: System crashes at 100M DAU
   - Mitigation: Design for 500M scale from Day 1, load testing
   - Budget: $50M infrastructure investment

2. **Competitor AI Superiority** (RED - 8/12)
   - Risk: Chess.com launches better AI
   - Mitigation: 18-month lead, $200M AI investment, continuous innovation
   - Budget: $200M AI/ML investment Phase J

3. **Privacy Breach** (RED - 8/12)
   - Risk: Hacker accesses 10M user accounts
   - Mitigation: Encryption, access controls, CISO, cyber insurance
   - Budget: $100M+ cyber liability insurance

4. **Key Executive Departure** (RED - 8/12)
   - Risk: CEO leaves unexpectedly
   - Mitigation: Succession planning, $10M key person insurance
   - Budget: Contingency planning

5. **Unit Economics Failure** (RED - 7/12)
   - Risk: CAC $5+, LTV $100 (breaks model)
   - Mitigation: Rigorous tracking, paywall optimization, viral mechanics
   - Budget: $50M growth optimization

6. **Market Demand Lower Than Projected** (YELLOW - 6/12)
   - Risk: Growth plateaus at 50M instead of 500M
   - Mitigation: User research, adjacent markets, geographic diversification
   - Budget: $30M market research

7. **Regulatory Restrictions** (YELLOW - 6/12)
   - Risk: App Store bans gaming
   - Mitigation: Legal compliance, alternative distribution, regional partnerships
   - Budget: $20M legal/compliance

8. **Talent Acquisition Challenges** (RED - 7/12)
   - Risk: Cannot hire 100+ top engineers
   - Mitigation: Competitive comp, remote-first, brand building
   - Budget: $50M recruitment/HR

9. **Team Culture Degradation** (RED - 6/12)
   - Risk: Toxic culture, high churn during 100x growth
   - Mitigation: Culture documentation, HR investment, leadership development
   - Budget: $100M culture/HR

10. **Data Loss/Corruption** (RED - 10/12)
    - Risk: Database corruption, backup failure
    - Mitigation: Multi-region backup, point-in-time recovery, audit logging
    - Budget: $50M disaster recovery
