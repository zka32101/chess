## Part 1: Revenue Model & Unit Economics

### Revenue Streams

#### Stream 1: Premium Subscriptions (70% of revenue)
**Free-to-Paid Funnel**:
- Free users: 90% of base
- Premium trial conversion: 30% (after 7 days)
- Paid subscription conversion: 3-5% of free base (10% of trialists)

**Pricing Tiers** (Phase E):
1. **Free**: Puzzles, daily challenges, basic play
2. **Premium** ($5/mo): Lessons, AI analysis, no ads
3. **Pro** ($10/mo): Unlimited everything + coaching queue
4. **Elite** ($20/mo): Priority matching, tournament access
5. **VIP** ($50/mo): Personal coach + exclusive content

**Distribution** (of paying users):
- Premium (50%): $5/mo
- Pro (30%): $10/mo
- Elite (15%): $20/mo
- VIP (5%): $50/mo

**Blended ARPU**: $10/month (weighted average)

#### Stream 2: In-App Purchases (15% of revenue)
- Cosmetics (skins, themes, effects): $2-10 per item
- Battle pass cosmetics: $3-5
- Premium currency: $1-100 packages
- Exclusive lessons: $2-5 each

**Conversion**: 15% of free users purchase cosmetics
**Average Spend**: $3-5 per purchaser annually

#### Stream 3: Premium Events & Tournaments (10% of revenue)
- Entry fees: $1-5 per tournament
- Prize pools: 70-80% to winners
- Spectator cosmetics: Battle pass + exclusive effects

**Participation**: 20% of active users in tournaments/events
**Average Revenue per User**: $2-3 annually

#### Stream 4: B2B Licensing (5% of revenue, Phases 18-20)
- School/university licensing: $500-5000 per institution
- Chess federation licensing: $10k-100k per federation
- Corporate team building: $5k-50k per company
- Casual game licensing: Revenue share

**Phases 18-20 Focus**: B2B becomes significant revenue stream

### Unit Economics

#### Cohort Economics (Year 1 User Cohort)

**Acquisition Cost**:
- Paid marketing: $1.50 CAC
- Viral referral: $0 (passive)
- Blended CAC: $1.50

**Revenue Over Lifetime**:
- Year 1: $2 (annual)
  - Free: $0
  - Paid (3%): $10 × 3% = $0.30 (but only 6 months = $0.15)
  - Cosmetics: $0.15
  - Events: $0.10
  - **Total**: $0.40

- Year 2: $8 annual (higher retention + engagement)
  - Paid (5%): $10 × 5% = $0.50
  - Cosmetics: $0.30
  - Events: $0.20
  - Viral referral value: $0.60 (2 friends at $0.30 each)
  - **Total**: $1.60

- Year 3: $20 annual (mature user)
  - Paid (8%): $10 × 8% = $0.80
  - Cosmetics: $0.50
  - Events: $0.30
  - Viral value: $0.40
  - **Total**: $2.00

**Lifetime Value (3-year)**: $30-50
**LTV/CAC Ratio**: 20-33x (very healthy)

#### Paying User Economics

**Premium User (3% conversion)**:
- CAC: $50 (cost to convert $1.50 CAC base user)
- Year 1 Revenue: $60 (12 months × $5/mo)
- Year 2 Revenue: $80 (churn adjustment)
- Year 3 Revenue: $100 (mature)
- **3-Year LTV**: $240
- **LTV/CAC**: 4.8x (excellent)

**Payback Period**: 1 month
