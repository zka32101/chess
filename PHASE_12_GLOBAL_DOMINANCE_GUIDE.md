## 8. Risk Management & Contingencies (Weeks 29-32)

### Key Risks & Mitigation

**1. M&A Integration Risk**
- Risk: Acquisition integration failures, culture clash
- Mitigation: Dedicated integration team, retained founders, gradual migration
- Contingency: 6-month integration timeline per acquisition, dedicated resources

**2. Technology Scaling Risk**
- Risk: Infrastructure failure during 5x scale-up
- Mitigation: Load testing, gradual rollout, multi-region failover
- Contingency: Dedicated DevOps team (30-50 people), real-time monitoring

**3. Regulatory Risk**
- Risk: Data privacy, child safety, gambling regulations
- Mitigation: Legal team in 25+ countries, compliance frameworks
- Contingency: $50M contingency for fines/settlements

**4. Market Competition**
- Risk: New entrants, existing platforms scaling
- Mitigation: Network effects, AI moat, patent protection
- Contingency: Aggressive product roadmap, continued innovation

**5. Talent Retention**
- Risk: Key talent loss, inability to scale hiring
- Mitigation: Competitive compensation, equity, culture
- Contingency: Signing bonuses for critical roles, retention agreements

**6. Foreign Exchange Risk**
- Risk: Currency fluctuations impact international revenue
- Mitigation: Natural hedges, currency derivatives
- Contingency: 10% reserve for FX losses
